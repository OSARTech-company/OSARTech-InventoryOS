Products and units

Create each item once, select its base stock unit, then add selling units such as packs, cartons, or bottles.

  • Use Units to define pack conversions.

Purchases and batches

Each purchase creates its own batch with a quantity, cost price, and selling price.

  • Enter the selling price for that delivery.
  • Old and new stock can retain different prices.
  • Choose the correct batch when selling.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers at a glance

Why can the same product have different selling prices?

Purchases are stored as batches. Each batch retains the price you set when it was received, so old and new stock can be sold at different prices.

What happens when I sell a pack instead of one item?

The selling unit converts the pack into the product’s base unit, then InventoryOS deducts the matching quantity from the selected batch.

Who can change a sale price?

Owners and managers can change it with a reason. Salespeople can apply a limited discount. Cashiers use the saved price.

How do I correct a wrong stock count?

Use Stock movements for a small correction, or Closing stock when you have completed a physical count of a store.
